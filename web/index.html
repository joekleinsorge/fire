WHAT THE MODEL INCLUDES

Visible assumptions, useful boundaries

Included
Account balances, real return estimates, recurring income and spending, scheduled cash flows, debt payments and a target based on your entered withdrawal rate.

Manual inputs required
After-tax income and spending, a flat tax rate on each account's withdrawals, benefits and medical costs.

Source-dependent
Statutory tax brackets, capital gains, RMDs and Roth conversions, government benefit estimates, healthcare programs, mortality tables and named withdrawal strategies are unavailable without reviewed source modules.

No big purchases in this plan yet. Try a scenario to see the trade-off.

Amounts are in today’s dollars. Purchases use available household cash and investments in the account withdrawal order; any borrowed amount becomes a fixed-payment loan, entered at its quoted rate and converted to today’s dollars with your expected inflation, whose payments start the month after the purchase. Upkeep also starts the month after and stops if the purchase is later sold. A purchase kept as an asset counts toward net worth but cannot be spent until a later purchase sells it; there is no property tax or tax deduction. Loan payments and upkeep that ends, where they fall after you retire, are added to your FI target as a lump sum. “Effect on your plan” compares the plan with and without each purchase. Enter school costs here only if they are not already covered by your education forecast or spending streams.
