      TEST THE LONG VIEW

      Retirement tests

      Choose a retirement age and test a fixed estimate or a range of market outcomes.

      Test your current plan

      The simulation uses the balances, cash flows, access choices, and assumptions in your current plan.

      Range of outcomes

      A simulation helps show uncertainty around the fixed estimate. Random markets are centered on it: half of simulated years compound above your return assumption and half below.

      ⌁

      Simulation results will appear here.

      Random markets use independent lognormal annual returns whose median is your return assumption. The bad-start test replaces the first three years of retirement with −15%, −13% and −24% real returns, roughly a 2000–2002 decline, then returns to your assumptions. Neither is a forecast or guarantee.

      WHAT THE MODEL INCLUDES

      Visible assumptions, useful boundaries

      Included
      Account balances, real return estimates, recurring income and spending, scheduled cash flows, debt payments and a target based on your entered withdrawal rate.

      Manual inputs required
      After-tax income and spending, benefits, medical costs and any tax assumptions you want represented.

      Source-dependent
      Statutory taxes, government benefit estimates, healthcare programs, mortality tables and named withdrawal strategies are unavailable without reviewed source modules.
